- “The exact independently reviewed planning package was produced within a documented runtime artifact window of about 66 minutes.”
- “The traditional comparable delivery is modeled at 28–64 person-hours, midpoint 44 hours.”
- “The conventional replacement-labor scenario is approximately $2.1k–$4.8k, midpoint about $3.3k, under disclosed assumptions.”

- “500× remains a target, not a claim.”

## What we do not claim
- We do not claim UBuildOS™ saved $2.1k–$4.8k in cash.
- We do not claim it definitely replaced exactly 44 human hours.
- We do not claim 66 minutes of model compute time.
- We do not claim the entire 30-day campaign is complete.
- We do not claim UBuildOS™ was 500× faster.

## Why
Actual project spend and an observed comparable historical baseline were not available. Modeled replacement cost, modeled compression, proven savings, revenue, valuation, and market price are different concepts.
